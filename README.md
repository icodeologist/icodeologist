### [Disaster-Watcher](https://github.com/icodeologist/Disaster-Watcher)

**A real-time local disaster reporting and notification backend, built in Go from scratch.**

Users post a report, the system finds people nearby, and notification workers deliver the alert. The interesting part is underneath: persistent job queues in PostgreSQL, typed worker pools, retries with exponential backoff, rate limiting, dead-letter handling, and graceful recovery after a crash.

`Go` · `PostgreSQL` · `Gin` · `Docker` · `concurrency` · `distributed-workflow thinking`
